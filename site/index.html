Open-source game & desktop streaming

Your PC. On your phone. One scan.

StreamBeam beams your Windows desktop and games to your phone over your own Wi‑Fi. Scan the QR code on your PC and you're playing in seconds — video, audio and controller, built for sub‑80 ms latency. No accounts. No cloud. No certificate warnings.

One QR code does everything

The QR on your PC is just a link. What happens when you scan it depends on what your phone needs next — there is no wrong first step.

app installed →

The link opens StreamBeam directly and the stream starts. Pairing is automatic.

no app yet →

The same link opens a page that gets you the app, then connects you. Scan once, not twice.

already paired →

Skip the QR entirely: your PC shows up in the app by itself when you're on the same network.

streambeam.eu/session/#sbm1:…

The part after # is your pairing code. It stays on your devices — browsers never send it to the server, and it expires after a few minutes. Pairing itself is end‑to‑end: the code contains your PC's public key, so no server ever sits in the middle.

Playing in three moves

STEP 1

Run StreamBeam on your PC

A small window shows a QR code. That's the whole setup — no browser config panel, no self-signed certificate warnings, no PIN.

STEP 2

Scan it with your phone

Use your camera or the app. Your phone and PC exchange keys directly over your Wi-Fi and remember each other from then on.

STEP 3

Play

Desktop, games, audio and controller input — streamed at full frame rate with hardware encoding on both ends.

Under the hood

Built in Rust, end to end. The same hardware pipeline the best remote-play tools use — with a protocol designed this decade.

LAN-only by design
Your pixels never leave your network. There is no relay, no account, nothing to subscribe to.
QUIC transport
Media rides unreliable datagrams — no head-of-line blocking when a packet drops mid-firefight.
Per-frame FEC
Reed–Solomon parity rebuilds lost packets without waiting for a retransmit.
Hardware codecs
NVENC / AMF / QuickSync on the PC, MediaCodec straight to the screen on your phone.
Pinned Ed25519 keys
Devices authenticate each other directly. No certificate authorities, no trust dialogs.
